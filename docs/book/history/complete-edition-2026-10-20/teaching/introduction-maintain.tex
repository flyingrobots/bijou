\chapter*{Keep the example reproducible}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction: Keep the example reproducible}

A useful application has a life after its first successful demonstration. These chapters record the edition, inspect its source package and walk through a deliberately bounded maintenance change.

\section*{What you will be able to do}

You should be able to reproduce the published checkpoint, describe what its evidence covers, and propose an upgrade without silently changing the reference edition.
