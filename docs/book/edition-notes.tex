\chapter*{About this edition}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this edition}

This edition contains 426 original source documents: 196 design-directory records and 230 guides, references, strategy notes, invariants, release documents, audits and historical records. New editorial material consists of the preface, part introductions and reading guidance. Source chapter bodies remain preserved; their arrangement and displayed chapter numbers follow the book's reading order.

All 32 Mermaid source diagrams are rendered as native TikZ. The component atlas contains 42 vector projections generated from actual DOGFOOD \texttt{Surface} cells at fixed times, viewports, variants and a shipped theme. The atlas documents those captures; the original sources and their hashes are listed in the final appendix.

Jacquard 24 is used for titles and headings, with IBM Plex Mono for prose and code. Both are distributed by \href{https://fonts.google.com}{Google Fonts}; their SIL Open Font Licenses accompany the book sources. DejaVu Sans supplies technical symbols absent from the primary fonts. The book's palette is anchored on \texttt{\#F7685F}; component artwork retains its actual application colours.

The source corpus is a frozen record of the retired Markdown documents. Selected emoji markers have readable text labels in the typeset edition, and workstation paths are normalized where applicable. Source paths, hashes and historical links provide provenance. They do not substitute for current tracker state or a fresh verification of runtime behaviour.
