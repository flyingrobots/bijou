\chapter*{About this edition}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this edition}

This edition contains 426 original source documents: 196 design-directory records and 230 guides, references, strategy notes, invariants, release documents, audits and historical records. New editorial material consists of the preface, eight part introductions, seven job-monitor workshops, a version page, a copyright page and a renderer gallery. The frozen source corpus preserves the original source bytes; typeset chapters replace conceptual arrow notation with figures and source tables with actual Bijou component renders.

All 32 Mermaid source diagrams are rendered as native TikZ. Another 83 source arrow figures and two new teaching traces illustrate flows, dependencies, state transitions and late results. All 123 tables, including the source inventory and two editorial tables, use the Bijou table component; wide and long tables are divided into 159 readable panels. The component atlas contains 42 vector projections generated from actual DOGFOOD \texttt{Surface} cells at fixed times, viewports, variants and a shipped theme. The atlas documents those captures; the original sources and their hashes are listed in the final appendix.

Jacquard 24 is used for titles and headings, with IBM Plex Mono for prose, VT323 for source listings and Recursive Mono for terminal specimens and component-rendered tables. These typefaces are distributed by \href{https://fonts.google.com}{Google Fonts}; their SIL Open Font Licenses accompany the book sources. DejaVu Sans supplies technical symbols absent from the primary fonts. The book's palette is anchored on \texttt{\#F7685F}; component artwork retains its actual application colours.

The source corpus is a frozen record of the retired Markdown documents. Selected emoji markers have readable text labels in the typeset edition, and workstation paths are normalized where applicable. Source paths, hashes and historical links provide provenance. They do not substitute for current tracker state or a fresh verification of runtime behaviour.
